TITLE
Art of Peace

TAGLINE
Plant trees. Guide them home.

DESCRIPTION
A tiny strategy game about keeping the peace. Collect seeds, plant trees, and guide rival armies through their color gates to bring people safely home.

CONTROLS
Hover: collect seeds. Left-click: plant. Right-click: remove.
Space: pause. F: speed. R: restart.
Desktop browser; mouse and keyboard.

CREDITS
Made with Godot. Sound effects by Kenney (CC0). AI-generated cover artwork.
